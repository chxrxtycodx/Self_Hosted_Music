# Music sourcing and copyright

## What this project is

A set of instructions and scripts for running **your own** music server and streaming **your own** files to **your own** devices. It does not include, host, distribute, or download any music, and nothing is shared publicly.

Navidrome, Tailscale, Amperfy, VLC, and SMB are general-purpose, legitimate tools.

## Recommended sources

| Source | Notes |
|---|---|
| [Bandcamp](https://bandcamp.com) | DRM-free downloads, MP3 or lossless; most money goes to the artist |
| [Qobuz](https://www.qobuz.com) | DRM-free lossless purchases |
| iTunes Store | DRM-free AAC purchases |
| Amazon Digital Music | DRM-free MP3 purchases |
| CDs | Rip your own with fre:ac or Exact Audio Copy (used CDs are cheap) |
| Free / Creative Commons | Artist-provided free downloads, Free Music Archive, Jamendo |

## About downloader tools (e.g. spotDL, yt-dlp)

Tools like **spotDL** match a streaming playlist to audio on YouTube and download it. They are **not used, bundled, or required** by this project, and none of the scripts here call them.

Downloading commercially released, copyrighted music from YouTube without permission is generally copyright infringement in the US, even for personal use, and also violates YouTube's Terms of Service. The audio is also lossy and lower quality than purchased files.

This project's maintainer builds their library from purchased and ripped music. If you choose to use third-party downloaders, you are responsible for making sure you have the rights to what you download.

**Playlist migration done right:** export your playlist as a track list (e.g. Exportify CSV), then use it as a shopping list to buy or rip the tracks.
